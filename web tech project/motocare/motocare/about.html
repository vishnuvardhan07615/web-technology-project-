Our Journey

Our Story

MotoCare originated in 2016 as an independent specialized tuning garage founded by veteran motorcycle mechanics. We noticed that two-wheeler riders regularly faced two extremes: expensive authorized dealerships with slow turnaround times, or unauthorized local roadside shops lacking digital diagnostic tools and genuine spares.

We set out to create a modern hybrid: a high-tech, multi-brand service facility where pricing is transparent, job cards are itemized, mechanics are master craftspeople, and customers receive honest advice without hidden charges.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, high-precision two-wheeler maintenance with upfront transparency, genuine parts, and unmatched turnaround speed.

Our Vision

To be the gold standard in urban two-wheeler care, seamlessly integrating future-ready EV diagnostics with classic mechanical mastery.
